## Gate Criteria (Must clear >= 4 of 6 dimensions)
1. **Act Count & Rhythm:** Avoids the standard 6-7 act pattern in the 13.6-13.8vh height band.
2. **The Peak Placement:** Engineered peak placed strategically in Act 3 (rather than Act 2 or predictable hero peak).
3. **Grammar Distinction:** Bespoke `Working-Surface Editorial Chapters` rather than generic single-page marketing river.
4. **Signature Move:** Authentic domain-specific interaction (`The Telemetry Vernier`, `data-sc-vernier`).
5. **Color & Material Token Floor:** Warm Espresso, Walnut, Vellum, and Amber palette (zero cold generic cyan/purple AI templates).
6. **Typographic & Layout Anchoring:** Deliberately varied anchors across acts (Left-lead -> Split-balanced -> Top-lead horizontal -> Asymmetric split-sticky -> Centered command dock).
