## 2. Grammar Selection: Working-Surface Editorial Chapters

Selected Grammar: **`Working-Surface Editorial Chapters`**

### Rejection Log for the Other 7 Candidate Grammars
1. **Continuous Narrative River:** Rejected because financial telemetry requires modular categorization. An unbroken single-column narrative stream disorients analysts looking for specific cross-asset correlations.
2. **Monolithic Pinned Canvas:** Rejected due to high GPU fill-rate penalties on mobile devices and rigid scroll-hijacking that frustrates institutional power users.
3. **Horizontal Ribbon Expressway:** Rejected because primary horizontal scrolling conflicts with standard desktop mouse-wheel expectations and hinders fast vertical comparisons.
4. **Split-Screen Dual Track:** Rejected because dual full-height scrolling tracks cause split-attention cognitive fatigue during high-volatility market sessions.
5. **Infinite Zoom Continuum:** Rejected because spatial zooming obscures quantitative threshold boundaries and feels like a consumer video game rather than a tier-one financial workstation.
6. **Card Stack Deck:** Rejected because stacked cards occlude preceding market context, preventing instantaneous cross-sector triangulation.
7. **Radial Astrolabe Carousel:** Rejected because circular layout coordinates impair fast columnar scanning of quotes, spreads, and percentage deltas.

## 3. Bespoke Signature Move: The Telemetry Vernier

* **Identifier:** `data-sc-vernier`
* **Mechanism:** An analog-calibrated vernier hair-rule tracking the active horizontal asset pan in Act 3. As the user navigates across the 32 asset classes, micro-tick sub-graduations calculate relative sector offsets and spotlight the active asset in warm terracotta ember (`#E06236`), displaying instantaneous delta telemetry and bid/ask spreads.
* **Fallback:** On viewports under 768px, the vernier seamlessly transitions into a sticky horizontal sector navigation bar with haptic-feel tactile indicators.
